FAQ

PR measurement, answered

What is PR measurement, and why does it matter?
PR measurement is the process of quantifying the impact of your communications strategy on your business value and brand reputation. It moves beyond counting clips to analyzing how your earned media coverage influences audience sentiment, narrative alignment, and corporate health.
How do you measure the ROI of public relations (PR)?
Connect your earned media data directly to business outcomes. Instead of arbitrary AVEs, focus on pipeline metrics, organic traffic growth, and changes in brand perception scores over time.
What is a Media Impact Score?
A holistic performance metric that factors in message pull-through, source authority, publication reach, and entity-level sentiment — a clear, benchmarked view of how specific articles shift public opinion.
How is share of voice calculated?
Traditionally, your percentage of media mentions within a peer set. Advanced tools now use a proprietary topic association score to measure your true connection to key narratives, regardless of raw article count.
What should PR reports include for the C-suite?
Executive-level reporting should focus on strategic data: a reputation scorecard against corporate pillars, competitive benchmarking, and forward-looking reputation risk intelligence.
How can AI help with PR measurement?
AI-powered analytics eliminate manual Boolean guesswork — summarizing complex datasets into C-suite briefs, categorizing entities, and accurately isolating sentiment to specific organizations.
Media monitoring vs. PR measurement — what's the difference?
Monitoring is reactive — capturing clips and mentions. PR measurement is a proactive branch of reputation management that turns raw coverage into structured external intelligence to guide narrative planning.
